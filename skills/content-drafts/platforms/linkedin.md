# LinkedIn drafts

Write in the founder's voice from the brand file: short paragraphs, a real point, and a clear next step. A person publishes the post.

## Length

The hard limit is 3,000 characters. Aim under 1,500. Put the point in the first line, and keep that line under about 210 characters so it shows before "see more". A longer first line is a warning. A post over 3,000 fails validation.

Three to five hashtags at the end is the usual range. More than five is a warning. Hashtags are optional when the audience does not use them.

## Shape

Open with the observation or the offer, then the detail, then one ask. A document or carousel is a brief the person builds. Buffer's CSV carries one text and one image, so a multi-image idea stays in the queue as a brief until the person has the file.

Mention an event by its in-file date and its event ID. Event Minder owns the announcement checklist. This draft is the social version of that moment.

## What to avoid

Skip engagement bait ("comment YES"), corporate filler, and any customer count, quote or launch date the brand file or event file does not state.
